The Azores Autonomy Decree of 1895 in Akoma Ntoso

The Decree of March 2, 1895, the first law to give the Azores a form of self-government. It holds the ministers’ report to the King, the decree of three articles, and the attached administrative organization of 63 articles. The document is marked up in Akoma Ntoso, the OASIS standard for legal and legislative documents.

View the decree

How it looks

The page sets the text as it was printed in the Collecção Official de Legislação Portugueza of 1895: the same six pages, the same two columns and the same line breaks, in a typeface of the period. The XML records every line and column break with the standard’s own eol and eop elements, so the words stay whole. It is reset type, not a facsimile.

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini. Claude read the text from the scan published by the Assembly of the Republic and checked it line by line against OCR. It didn’t write a word of the text, and the old spelling is kept.
