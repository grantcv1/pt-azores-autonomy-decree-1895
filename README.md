# The Azores Autonomy Decree of 1895 in Akoma Ntoso

The Portuguese Decree of March 2, 1895, in Akoma Ntoso XML, with a stylesheet and a page that set it line for line as it was printed in 1895. It was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini.

The decree let any Azorean district adopt a new administrative organization if two-thirds of its citizens eligible for administrative office asked for it. It is the first law to give the Azores a form of self-government.

## About the markup

- **Standard:** Akoma Ntoso 2.0 (AKN 3.1). The namespace is `http://docs.oasis-open.org/legaldocml/ns/akn/3.0`, with `version="3.1"` on the root element.
- **Validation:** The XML validates against the Akoma Ntoso 2.0 cs01 schema.
- **Structure:** An `<act>` holds the ministers' report in `<preamble>`, the enacting formula, Articles 1 to 3 in `<body>`, and the King's and ministers' signatures in `<conclusions>`. The administrative organization is an `<attachment>` holding its own `<act>`, with `<chapter>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<paragraph>`, `<list>` and `<point>` elements.
- **Line and page breaks:** `<eol>` and `<eop>` record each printed line and column end. Where a line divides a word, the marker sits before the word with `breakAt` and `breakWith`, as the schema directs, so the text stays searchable. An `<eop>` number such as `311a` means page 311, first column, because the standard has no column element.
- **People:** The ministers are `<person>` elements that point to the `<references>` block.
- **Identifiers:** Every element with an `eId` also has a `GUID`. Text-level `<p>`, `<intro>` and `<content>` elements have neither.
- **Cross-references:** Internal references to articles in the attachment are `<ref>` elements. References to the Código Administrativo and other laws are plain text.

The FRBR URIs use the jurisdiction `pt`, because this is a national act.

## About the rendition

`view.html` rebuilds the six printed pages from the XML, using its line and column markers. It runs numbers into the first line and sets each line to the column width.

- The typeface is Old Standard TT, a modern revival of type of this kind. It isn't the original type, so this is not a facsimile.
- The running heads ("Março 2", "310", "1895") are on the printed pages but are not part of the decree, so the page adds them and the XML leaves them out.
- Pages 310 and 315 also carry other acts. Those are left out and marked `[…]`.

## How the text was made

There was no text source. Claude read the 721 lines from the scan, then compared them with Tesseract OCR of the same images. 714 lines matched closely. The other 7 were checked and are OCR misreadings. Nobody fluent in 19th-century Portuguese has proofread it, so small errors may remain.

## Left as they are

The spelling is that of 1895. These apparent errors in the printing were kept:

| Where | As printed | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annex, Art. 27 | `commisssões` | Three s's |
| Annex, Art. 32, § 1 | Items run 8.º, then 10.º | No item 9.º |
| Annex, Art. 32, § 2 | `falcultativas` | For `facultativas` |
| Annex, Art. 57 | `ja` and `já` | Both spellings appear |
| Annex, Art. 53 | `commissão executiva` | Elsewhere `commissão districtal` |
